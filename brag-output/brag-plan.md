# Brag Plan: Faisal Hospital AI Receptionist

## Planning Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   Faisal Hospital AI Receptionist is an intelligent 24/7 medical voice and chat assistant that provides verified hospital knowledge grounding, red-flag emergency triage, and instant real-time doctor appointment bookings.

2. **What is the most impressive claim?**
   "Always on. Zero wait times. Books verified specialist appointments in 15 seconds flat."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   Deep medical teal/emerald dark mode (`#04151f`, `#0d9488`, `#14b8a6`) with pulsating neural voice rings, real-time waveform bars, and an authentic confirmed appointment card with reference badge `APT-1045`.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   - The AI Receptionist voice call screen with live waveform visualizer and streaming chat.
   - The instant verified booking card: `Dr. Nadia Ali (Dermatology)`, `Tomorrow at 11:45 AM`, `APT-1045`.
   - The hospital operations dashboard with real-time appointments queue and analytics metrics.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   - Trailer: 18.0 seconds (`brag.mp4`)
   - Master Launch Video: 92.5 seconds (`brag_final.mp4`) combining cinematic intro and live voice demo.

6. **Tone preset and creative direction:**
   - Preset: `polished`
   - Direction: Premium medical AI launch trailer + authentic live voice demo.

7. **Audio feel:**
   Clean modern tech bed (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`) with soft UI click accents and a satisfying confirmation chime.

8. **Share caption:**
   "Meet the AI Receptionist we built for Faisal Hospital: 24/7 patient triage, natural voice, and instant verified doctor bookings in real time."

9. **User flow shown:**
   Patient asks to book Dr. Nadia Ali -> AI checks availability and offers 11:45 AM -> Appointment confirmed with reference APT-1045 -> Pushed to live hospital queue.
